# Sample artwork for development

Posters and stills of the Blender Foundation's open movies, used in the mockups and for development. Each image is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution, which allows use and changes as long as the credit below is kept with it.

These were taken from [MyTVPlayer's demo art](https://github.com/biskyt/MyTVPlayer/tree/main/app/src/test/resources/demo-art), which took them from Wikimedia Commons, cropped and resized them to 360 × 540 (posters) and 960 × 540 (stills), and checked each licence.

**Changes made here:** the backdrops for Charge, Cosmos Laundromat, Elephants Dream, Sintel and Tears of Steel were made from their posters (cropped to 16:9, blurred and darkened). The two other backdrops are the original stills.
