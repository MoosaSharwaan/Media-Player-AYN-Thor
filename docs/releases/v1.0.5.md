Playback info, one card per show, and a tidier Anime tab.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.5.apk` below and open it on the Thor. It installs over v1.0.1 and later and keeps everything.

## What's new

- **Playback info on the paused screen.** Pause a film and the bottom of the top screen shows what is decoding it and how it's going:
  `Video  FFmpeg mpeg4 · 720×400 · 23.98 fps · 3 dropped`
  `Audio  c2.android.mp3.decoder · EN · Stereo · MP3`
  plus "waited N×" when playback had to stop for the file. It tells a slow decoder (dropped frames) from a slow file (waits) or troublesome sound.
- **One card per show, whatever its files call it.** "Enen no Shouboutai - S02E23" and "Fire Force - S03E01" are the same series under its Japanese and English names. Once ⟳ Fetch info finds them as the same show on TMDB, they become one Fire Force card, and it fetches the episodes it gained. Different shows in one folder (Naruto and Shippuden) stay apart.
- **Each season looks like itself.** Focusing a season fills the top screen with that season's poster: the whole poster at full height on the right, dissolving into a blurred copy of itself behind the text. No more framed poster in front of the show's background. ⟳ Fetch info saves a large copy of each season's poster for this.
- **Extras with careless spacing stay off the tabs too:** "Bungo Stray Dogs S2- Opening 2". Episodes named "Show S2- 05" are read as episodes.
- **Diagnostics** (Select + Start) starts with the app's version, so a photo of it says which build is installed.

## If an AVI still lags

Play it for a minute where it lags, pause, and send a photo of the top screen. "FFmpeg" in the Video line means the new decoder is in use; a `c2.` name means it isn't.

## After updating

Press ⟳ Fetch info once: shows already fetched under two names merge then.
