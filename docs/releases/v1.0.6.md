Libraries, previews behind the focused movie or episode, and tabs on L1 / R1.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.6.apk` below and open it on the Thor. It installs over v1.0.1 and later and keeps everything.

## What's new

- **Libraries.** Keep films and work apart: Settings › Libraries makes a library, names it ("Entertainment", "Work"), and gives it one or more folders. Each library has its own titles, Continue watching and watched marks. Browsing shows one at a time: **L2** switches, and each library remembers its tab and card. Deleting a library never touches the videos on the card. Your current library becomes the first one, unchanged.
- **Previews.** Rest on a movie, an Others video, a Continue watching card or an episode in a season for a second, and moments of it play on the top screen behind its title and description, which stay on top. A new moment every 12 seconds, picked from the middle of the file: past the intro, never the ending.
- Series and season cards keep their art: a preview plays a file, so it starts when you reach an episode.
- **Settings › Browsing:** Previews (on by default) and Preview sound (off by default).
- Previews use their own player: they never change where you stopped, never take the sound from anything else, and stop the moment you open something or leave the app.

- **Shoulder buttons swapped:** L1 / R1 now switch tabs, skip 10 s in the player and switch Movie / Series in Fix match; R2 opens the folder browser. (These were L2 / R2 and R1.)

## Battery

A preview decodes video while you browse, which uses more battery than a still poster. Turn Previews off in Settings to save it.
