A tidier library: extras off the tabs, and one show per series.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.2.apk` below and open it on the Thor (or `adb install -r exo-player-v1.0.2.apk`). It installs over v1.0.1 and keeps your keys, library and progress. Coming from v1.0.0, uninstall that first (see v1.0.1).

## What's new

- **Extras stay off the tabs.** Openings, endings and other bonus files no longer fill the Anime tab as titles: anything in an extras folder ("Extras", "S1 Extras", Bonus, Featurettes…), and files named Opening, Ending, NCOP, NCED, Creditless, Trailer, PV or Preview with no episode number. They're still playable from the folder browser (R1).
- **One show per series.** "Bungo Stray Dogs S2 - 05" is season 2, episode 5 of Bungo Stray Dogs, so its seasons sit under one card. "Season 2 - 05", "2nd Season - 05" and season folders like "Overlord - S1" work the same way.
- **Anime films named for Fetch info:** "Bungo Stray Dogs - Movie 01 - Dead Apple" is "Bungo Stray Dogs: Dead Apple".
- **The library fixes itself:** every scan reads the names again, so cards filed wrongly by v1.0.1 are corrected when the app opens. Press ⟳ Fetch info once for the merged shows' posters and seasons.

## Tested

94 tests on a PC, including a library laid out like the Thor's (Bungo Stray Dogs with its openings and endings, Overlord with S1 Extras): three cards, no extras, and a rescan that fixes files filed by v1.0.1.

## Known limits

- The v1 gates (on-device test, battery against VLC) are still to run on the Thor.
- Not in v1: MKV chapter jumps, a touch lock, notification controls.
