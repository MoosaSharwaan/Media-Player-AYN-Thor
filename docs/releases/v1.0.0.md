The first usable version of Exo Player: a video player for the AYN Thor's two screens. The film plays on the top screen with nothing over it; everything you touch is on the bottom screen.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.0.apk` below, copy it to the Thor and open it (or `adb install exo-player-v1.0.0.apk`). Pick your video folder when the app asks, then add your API keys in Settings (Start). See the [README](https://github.com/MoosaSharwaan/Media-Player-AYN-Thor#getting-started) for where to get the keys.

## What's in it

- **Library:** your SD card folder, names like `Breaking.Bad.S02E05.mkv` and `[SubsPlease] Frieren - 12.mkv` read automatically, five tabs (Continue watching, Movies, Series, Anime, Others), seasons and episodes, search, sort and filter, a folder browser.
- **Watching:** resume where you stopped, Up next with a countdown, audio and subtitle tracks, subtitle timing, a volume bar with boost to 200 %, speed, picture modes, audio delay, subtitle look, brightness. Leaving the app pauses the film and frees its decoders.
- **Online only when you ask:** ⟳ Fetch info (TMDB, fanart.tv, AniList; only what's missing; finishes in the background), ✎ Fix match, Find subtitles (OpenSubtitles, saved next to the video). Keys are stored encrypted on the device.

## Known limits

- Not yet tested on a Thor: the two v1 gates (on-device test, battery against VLC) are next.
- TMDB, fanart.tv and OpenSubtitles have only been tested against stand-in servers; AniList was tested live.
- A debug build, signed with a debug key. A later version signed with a different key needs this one uninstalled first, which clears the library and settings.
- Not in v1: MKV chapter jumps, a touch lock, notification controls.
