Fixes from the first look at v1.0.0, and the battery on the bottom screen.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.1.apk` below, copy it to the Thor and open it (or `adb install -r exo-player-v1.0.1.apk`).

**If you installed v1.0.0, uninstall it first.** v1.0.0 was signed with a one-off key; from v1.0.1 on every release uses the same key, so later updates install over this one and keep your library and settings.

## What's new

- **Subtitles inside the video file that are pictures now show.** Blu-ray (PGS) and DVD (VobSub) subtitles were found but never drawn. They now appear over the film, where the disc places them, and follow the picture mode. Text subtitles inside the file (SRT, ASS, MP4) already worked.
- **Turning subtitles off applies to the open film only.** Before, the next films started with subtitles off too, until the app closed.
- **Audio and subtitle tracks are easier to tell apart:** "EN · 5.1 · AC3 · Commentary", "Stereo · FLAC", "EN · PGS". Tracks with no language no longer show "UND".
- **The bottom screen leaves with the app.** Minimising the app left its window on the bottom screen.
- **Battery percentage on the bottom screen,** next to the corner buttons: red at 15 % and below, ⚡ while charging.
- **Diagnostics** (Select + Start) lists the audio formats the app's FFmpeg plays when the Thor has no decoder of its own.

## Tested

92 tests on a PC, now including real clips played through the player: SRT, ASS, MP4 text and PGS subtitles; AAC, AC3, E-AC3, DTS, TrueHD, FLAC, Opus, MP3, Vorbis and PCM audio tracks; the bottom window leaving and coming back with the app.
