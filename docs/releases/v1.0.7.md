Private libraries with a passkey, L2 + R3 to switch library, tidying up after videos you remove, and a fix for the first open.

## Install

Download `exo-player-v1.0.7.apk` below and open it on the Thor. It installs over v1.0.1 and later and keeps everything.

## What's new

- **L2 + R3 switches library.** Hold L2 and press R3 (either order). L2 alone no longer switches, so it can't happen by accident.
- **A passkey for a library.** In Settings › Libraries, open a library and choose Set passkey: a combo of 4 to 8 presses on the D-pad, A, X and Y, entered twice, then ▷ Start. Switching to it, or opening it in Settings, asks for the combo; L2 + R3 again skips past it.
- When you leave the app, a locked library locks again: Browsing goes back to your first open library, and a video from it closes after saving your place.
- One library always stays without a passkey. Forgot one? On the list in Settings › Libraries, press X twice on it to delete that library; its videos stay on the card.
- **Removing videos frees their space.** When a video is gone at the next scan, what belonged to it goes too: its title, season and episode details, posters, backdrops and stills, subtitles kept in the app, and where you stopped. The same happens when you delete a library or remove a folder from one.
- **Kept on purpose:** your Fix match choices, so a video that comes back is matched the same way, and subtitles saved next to your videos on the card.
- **Settings › Artwork and subtitles** shows the space they take on the Thor; A clears anything left by videos that are gone straight away.
- A folder that can't be read (the SD card out) still removes nothing.
- **Fixed: the app minimized on first open.** Opened from the bottom screen, it went straight to recents and had to be reopened from there. It now opens on the top screen first time.

## Tested

132 tests on a PC, including a show removed from a library: its details, artwork, app-kept subtitle and history go; the other show and the Fix match choice stay. Also L2 + R3 switching past and into a locked library, a wrong and a right passkey, locking again on Home, and that the icon starts the player in its own task and the launch step closes.
